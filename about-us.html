What we are building

Useful work should be
easier to find.

Our goal is a clear, accessible way for companies to get help with practical tasks and for independent workers to find opportunities that fit their skills, availability and location. The platform is in development; this site presents the concept and planned user journeys.

✦

The problem we want to solve

Complementary
by design.

We are starting with small, time-sensitive needs that are often declined because they take too long to arrange or do not justify a provider’s travel. A construction company, for example, might need short-term help organizing tools on a site.

Q DEAL is intended to complement employees, specialist providers and temporary-work agencies. Missions should have a clear scope, and independent workers should be free to choose which opportunities and clients to pursue.

A profile that works for you

Skills, area and availability.

Independent workers will be able to describe their skills, choose a travel area and set availability. Guided onboarding is one of the options we are considering to make profiles more useful and mission suggestions more relevant.

What guides the product

Our priorities.

The next features will be shaped by useful feedback from both businesses and independent workers.

01 / Useful

Start with a real need.

  • Keep the task practical.
  • Describe what needs doing.
  • Make the next step clear.
02 / Nearby

Make distance part of the match.

  • Show the location up front.
  • Respect the worker’s area.
  • Bring small needs closer.
03 / Independent

Keep choice with the worker.

  • Make it easy to review a mission.
  • Let workers decide to respond.
  • Support multiple client relationships.
04 / Clear

Build trust through context.

  • Use descriptions and photos.
  • Set expectations early.
  • Learn from feedback over time.
