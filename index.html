Good to know

Questions and
answers.

What is Q DEAL?

Q DEAL is a marketplace concept connecting businesses with independent workers for clearly scoped, hands-on missions. It is intended to complement existing employees and staffing agencies.

What kind of tasks can be listed?

Examples include sorting tools or materials, helping prepare a store or event, basic stock organization, and certain concierge or common-area tasks. Any mission should match the worker’s skills and the rules that apply to the activity.

How will task estimates work?

A business will be able to describe a request and add photos. The estimate is intended as a first guide; the business reviews the details and confirms the final mission scope and terms.

How will nearby matching work?

Independent workers can set their location, travel area, skills and availability. Q DEAL is being designed to use those details to suggest relevant nearby missions.

How does Q DEAL support independent work?

The concept is built around defined missions and the worker’s ability to choose whether to respond and which clients to work with. The platform is not a guarantee of legal status; every working relationship must comply with the applicable rules.

Will there be training and a chatbot?

Those are planned features: short online videos for practical tasks and a chatbot to answer common questions from both businesses and independent workers.

What additional features are being considered?

Possible additions include business ratings after missions, separate business and worker interfaces, and guided onboarding to improve mission suggestions.
