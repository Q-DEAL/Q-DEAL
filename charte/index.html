Frequently asked questions

Questions and
answers.

What is Q DEAL?

Q DEAL is a marketplace that connects businesses with self-employed workers for clearly scoped, hands-on tasks. It complements existing teams and staffing agencies.

What kinds of tasks can be posted?

Examples include organizing site materials, preparing a store or event, and some concierge or shared-area tasks. Each task must fit the worker’s skills and comply with the rules that apply.

How do photo-based estimates work?

The estimate uses a written brief and photos to provide an initial guide. The business then confirms the final scope and terms.

How do local task suggestions work?

Your profile includes skills, travel area and availability so Q DEAL can suggest relevant nearby tasks.

Does Q DEAL replace employees or staffing agencies?

No. Q DEAL complements employees, specialist providers and staffing agencies. A platform cannot determine employment status on its own; each working relationship must comply with applicable rules.

Do you offer training and an assistant?

Yes: short training videos on practical tasks, and a conversational assistant for businesses and workers.

What else does Q DEAL offer?

Post-task reviews, separate business and worker spaces, and guided profile setup.
