A complementary way to work

Support for tasks
that fall between the gaps.

A crew may need help organizing tools, a store may need a brief stock reset, or a property team may need support with a defined common-area task. Frequently asked questions

Questions and
answers.

Is Q DEAL available yet?

No. Q DEAL is in development. This site introduces the concept and planned features; accounts and live task listings are not available yet.

What kinds of tasks are being considered?

Examples under consideration include organizing site materials, preparing a store or event, and some concierge or shared-area tasks. Each task must fit the worker’s skills and comply with the rules that apply.

How will photo-based estimates work?

The planned feature will use a written brief and photos to provide an initial guide. It is not available yet, and the business will need to confirm the final scope and terms.

How will local task suggestions work?

The planned profile will include skills, travel area and availability so Q DEAL can suggest relevant nearby tasks. Local matching is not active yet.

Does Q DEAL replace employees or staffing agencies?

No. Q DEAL is intended to complement employees, specialist providers and staffing agencies. A platform cannot determine employment status on its own; each working relationship must comply with applicable rules.

Which features are planned?

Short training videos and a conversational assistant are being considered. Neither feature is available yet.

What other features are being considered?

Other ideas include post-task reviews, separate business and worker spaces, and guided profile setup.
